Privacy Policy for MedDhatri

Last Updated: November 04, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MedDhatri (“we”, “our”, or “us”). MedDhatri operates the MedDhatri mobile application, a dedicated recruitment and career management platform for healthcare and medical professionals (including doctors, nurses, pharmacists, and allied health staff).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal and professional information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with applicable Indian data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).

By accessing or using the MedDhatri mobile application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide healthcare job matching, application tracking, and credential verification services, we collect:

A. Personal & Professional Profile Information

  • Identity Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and profile photograph.
  • Professional & Educational Credentials: Medical qualification details (MBBS, MD, MS, B.Sc Nursing, etc.), college/institution attended, graduation year, clinical experience, and specialization.
  • Council Registrations: State Medical Council or National Medical Commission (NMC) registration numbers provided for credential verification.
  • Resumes & Documents: CVs, cover letters, and certification documents voluntarily uploaded by you.

B. Application & Communication Data

  • Job Applications: Records of job vacancies you view, save, or apply for.
  • Recruiter Communications: Messages and interview schedules exchanged with verified hospital recruiters or healthcare hiring teams through the platform.

C. Device & Technical Information

  • Device manufacturer, model, and operating system version.
  • App performance, crash reports, and basic analytics to improve platform stability.
  • Push notification tokens (to deliver job alerts and interview updates).
Explicit Note: MedDhatri does NOT collect patient medical records, diagnostic test results, health vitals, or clinical treatment data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate recruitment and professional networking purposes:

  • To create and maintain your professional clinician/candidate account.
  • To match your clinical profile and specialization with relevant healthcare job vacancies.
  • To transmit your job applications and uploaded CVs to prospective hospital employers.
  • To facilitate interview scheduling and in-app communications between you and hospital HR teams.
  • To verify professional medical credentials and maintain platform integrity.
  • To send important job notifications, interview reminders, and account security updates.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only in the following scenarios:

  • Prospective Employers & Hospitals: When you apply for a job vacancy, your professional profile, resume, and credentials are shared with that specific hospital or clinic hiring team.
  • Service Providers: Trusted infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting, database management, and push notification services) operating under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by applicable Indian law, court order, or governmental authorities.

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All network communication is encrypted using secure TLS/SSL protocols.
  • Access Controls: Access to candidate information is strictly restricted to authorized personnel and verified hiring institutions.
  • Data Location: Data is securely stored in compliance with Indian data sovereignty standards.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal and professional profile data for as long as your account remains active.

Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can update your details anytime in the app. You may also request complete deletion of your account and associated resume/profile data by contacting us at our support email (support@meddhatri.in) or via in-app profile settings. Upon verified request, your personal data will be deleted within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

MedDhatri is a professional career and employment platform intended exclusively for adult medical and healthcare professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted within the app or on our website with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
